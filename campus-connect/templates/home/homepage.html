About

Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (RGUKT) was established to provide high-quality technical education to rural youth. With fully residential campuses, RGUKT offers a unique 6-year integrated program (PUC + B.Tech), along with advanced postgraduate and research opportunities.

Guided by its vision to bridge the rural-urban education gap, RGUKT nurtures innovation, research, and holistic development to empower students for a brighter future.
