UNDERSTAND THE PLATFORM

What each space is
made for.

Each part of Campus Connect has a clear purpose, keeping information useful without making the experience overwhelming.

01 / STUDENT COMMUNITY

Clubs

Discover club announcements, activities, events and opportunities from student communities across campus.

02 / CAMPUS ORGANIZATIONS

Cells

Find programs, initiatives and updates from the different cells working across the campus community.

03 / LEARNING

Academic

Keep useful academic information, announcements and important notices easier to discover.

04 / CAMPUS LIFE

Non-Academic

Explore activities, events and experiences that make student life extend beyond the classroom.

05 / ESSENTIAL

Emergency

Keep essential emergency contacts and information accessible when quick action matters.

06 / STUDENT VOICE

Complaints

Provide a clearer route for concerns, feedback and student support across the campus.

07 / CAMPUS VOICE

Radio

Give campus audio, announcements and student voices another engaging way to reach the community.

08 / STAY UPDATED

Reminders

Keep important dates, deadlines and notices visible so students can stay prepared.
